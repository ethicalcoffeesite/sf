Why "ethical coffee"? Coffee is one of the many industries that has a high risk for human and environmental exploitation. Again and again, major suppliers of coffee are caught using individuals trafficked and literally enslaved, with or without the literal consent of the governments of the countries the coffee is grown in. You might have heard that there is "no slavery anymore," but this is not true. Often under the guise of "debts," slavers seek to legitimize their control over enslaved individuals and their children (who are "passed the debt" instead of "born into slavery"- a linguistic slip which has no bearing on the fact that yes, children are literally being born into intergenrational slavery to this day and we all support it when we purchase many brands of coffee...). Enslaved people on coffee plantations in Latin America and Africa are beaten if they refuse to work, terrorized with threats of extreme violence, deprived of adequate food, clean water, medical care and education, or the right to freely sell their labor (yay). It is unthinkable that this is what we have been consuming, but it is something that must be faced if something is going to change. Slavery isn't "over" or some relic of the past. It continues to be an essential part of the global economy, and an essential part of many of the products that you consume every day. Becuase it isn't just coffee. It's the cheap, "guiltless" binges on cheap and luxury clothing brands alike (forever 21, nike, etc). It's agricultural products from chocolate to beef to watermelon and tomato. It's the lithium in cell phones and the jemstones that might just be on your wedding ring. And it's coffee, too. And you might be overwhelmed and you might say I might as well not care about anything or anyone but I want you to look at this map. There are so many shops that have chosen to do the right thing (human rights are marked in greem, ecological only is marked with purple). It is a choice. It is a very doable choice. Some brands will try to make you feel like this is a difficult choice and that you are powerless but you are not. Look at this map. Let the buisnesses that profit on human suffering go out of buisness. Do not accept the lie that there is something enevitable about slavery. Choose a world without that lie.

A note on method: Information is gathered by scanning publicly listed coffee shop websites on Google for key terms related to sustainability, direct trade, fair wages, and ethical labor standards. A "hit" on this list doesn't necessarily mean that all products sold by the shop are beyond a shadow of a doubt ethical, only that these shops have reported carrying ethical options. Other ethical coffee options do exist. This website WILL miss any coffee shop without a website, and WILL miss coffee shops with websites that lack information about coffee sourcing.

(Why is my coffee shop not on here? We try so hard!) I'm sorry! This website will update semi-regularly, so if you 1. ensure your website is working 2. ensure your website is connected to your google maps listing and 3. ensure your website describes, in plain and publically visible text, the ethical component of your coffee. Do not only list the brand and assume the machine will be able to make the jump (or any of us humans, for that matter). Transparency should be the new norm.
